# Privacy policy: Murugan Uploader

Murugan Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels
"BrahmaNadagam" and "அருள் குட்டீஸ்" (the kids' channel) to upload and schedule that owner's own videos.

## What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to manage the owner's own
YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to:

- upload the owner's videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
- schedule them and add them to the owner's playlists;
- read the owner's own channel details and video statistics.

## What it does with data

- It has no users other than the channel owner and collects no data about anyone else.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private GitHub
  Actions secrets, and is used only for the uploads described above.

## Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
